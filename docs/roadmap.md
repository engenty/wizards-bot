## The phone as a place where work happens

What a phone keeps stays on it; its own model works on it, the home's agents hand it questions
(`device_ask`), and only answers travel. Built: the phone's tools (calendar, reminders, contacts,
folders connected on the phone, services signed in on the phone), hand-offs with the person's yes
per agent on the phone (the home learns it and offers the phone only to agents it did not
refuse), a silent push that wakes the app for a hand-off, links into apps, calls and message
drafts the person starts on the phone, and the list of what was asked. Planned next:

| Feature                                  | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Depends on                    |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Sign-in for a service on the phone       | A service on the phone takes a token today. OAuth: the phone registers itself with the service (dynamic client registration), signs in in its own sign-in browser and returns to `wizardsbot://`; the tokens and their renewal stay in the phone's Keychain.                                                                                                                                                  | a service that asks for OAuth |
| Location and weather on the phone        | A `location` resource: where the phone is now, while the app is in use only (no "Always": 5.1.5 asks for location only where it is directly relevant, and 2.5.4 limits background use to its purpose), rounded to a district unless the question needs more. Weather from WeatherKit on the phone; every place that shows it names Apple Weather and links Apple's data sources, as the WeatherKit terms ask. | —                             |
| Photos on the phone                      | Pictures the person picks in the system's picker (no full library access: 5.1.1(iii) prefers the out-of-process picker). The phone's model reads them on the phone; a picture leaves only like a file today, after a yes.                                                                                                                                                                                     | —                             |
| Music and Shortcuts                      | Music by MusicKit on the phone (what plays, play a playlist), asked like any change. App Intents both ways: "Ask Goose" from Siri and Shortcuts goes to the home (or the phone's model when the home is out of reach); automations of the Shortcuts app (arriving at a place, a time) start a task of an agent through an intent.                                                                             | —                             |
| Health                                   | Only for a health feature the person asks for (2.5.1: HealthKit for health and fitness, with the Health app). Health data is never used for anything else and never goes to a third party (5.1.2(vi), 5.1.3(i)); nothing of it is kept in iCloud (5.1.3(ii)); an answer that holds health data asks before it leaves the phone, every time.                                                                   | a health feature              |
| Home devices                             | Not on the phone: HomeKit is for apps whose service is home automation (2.5.1) and its data may not be used for anything else (5.1.2(vi)). Home devices are reached at the home, as a connection (Home Assistant over MCP).                                                                                                                                                                                   | —                             |
| Mail, calendars and contacts at the home | For a home that runs on a server: mail over IMAP (the channel's reading part, as a connection), Google and Microsoft calendars and contacts over their MCP servers, and iCloud calendars and contacts over CalDAV and CardDAV with an app-specific password kept in `Secrets`. These are the home's own connections, beside what each phone keeps.                                                            | connections                   |

Checked against the App Review Guidelines of October 16, 2026 (read on Apple's site on 2026-11-21): 5.1.1, 5.1.2, 5.1.3, 5.1.5, 2.5.1, 2.5.4. WeatherKit's attribution is in Apple's WeatherKit pages, not in the guidelines. Calls and messages: 5.1.2(v) allows contacting someone from the contacts only at the person's own initiative, one at a time, showing what the message says before it is sent; the phone does exactly that, and never sends itself.
